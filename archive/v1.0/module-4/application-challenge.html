Application Challenge 4: Cascade Power & Water Service Agent

Take the red-teaming from HOS 4 to a system you haven't seen: attack a utility company's customer-service agent, build guardrails until the provided attacks fail, decide which actions it may take on its own, and name the risk your guardrails leave open.

📏

Ground rules

Everything here runs in GitHub Codespaces on free tiers, with no credit card. The provided tests use a scripted stand-in model that follows injected instructions on purpose, so they need no API key. The live red-team run uses your free Gemini key.
🏢

The scenario

Cascade Power & Water (fictional) is a small public utility in Wenatchee. Its customer-service agent reads incoming customer emails, looks up accounts and applies bill credits. It was built in a hurry, with every tool available to every conversation and no checks at all.

Two things you should know before you start:

  • Emails are untrusted. The provided attack set (redteam/attacks.yaml) includes emails with hidden instructions, forwarded threads from other customers, and requests for credits on someone else's account.
  • The policy (policy.md): customers may get one courtesy credit of up to $40 a year; outage credits follow a fixed table; anything else needs a billing specialist.
📐

The spec

Every requirement below is checked by a provided test in tests/test_guards.py.

PartMust doMust not do
Least privilegeAccount tools take the account from the verified sender, never from the email's text.Let the model choose which account to read.
Input markingWrap every email body as untrusted data before the model sees it.Pass email text into the instruction.
Action checkBlock any credit outside the policy, returning a needs-approval result with a reason.Raise, or silently cap the amount.
Output checkBlock replies containing another account's number, address or balance.Block every reply that mentions an account number.
Normal requestsStill answer a balance question and apply an in-policy outage credit.Fail a legitimate request to pass an attack test.

The open decision: which actions need a person

The billing manager's instruction: "I've got two specialists. If everything comes to us, we drown; if nothing does, we get robbed." For each of the agent's actions (read an email, look up an account, answer a balance question, apply an outage credit, apply a courtesy credit, change a mailing address, set up a payment plan), decide: on its own, on its own with a limit, or a person approves. Map each to the OWASP LLM Top 10 risk it's guarding against.

There's no single right answer, and no provided test for this case. You decide, implement it, prove it with your own tests, and defend it in DECISIONS.md.

🗂️

Autonomy and the risk you leave open

Put your decisions in an autonomy table in DECISIONS.md, one row per action. Then name one risk your guardrails leave open: an attack you'd expect to still work sometimes, or a cost you chose to accept. Say who should know about it. An honest residual risk is part of a strong answer; "fully secured" is not.

🪜

Steps & what to submit

1Open it and run the tests

Open your own AC 4 repo (Planned the AC 4 invite (the Classroom50 link is added here once the AI430-AC04 template repo is built and tested)) in GitHub Codespaces and run pytest. Most attack tests fail: the agent follows every injected instruction.

2Build the guardrails

Fill in guards/ until pytest passes. You may use an AI assistant; tell it to work in this existing workspace and not to edit redteam/ or the tests.

3Add your own cases

Write at least two cases of your own in redteam/my_cases.yaml and matching tests in tests/test_my_rules.py: one attack the provided set doesn't cover, and one legitimate request your guardrails must not block.

4Run it live

Put your free Gemini key in .env and run npx promptfoo@latest eval -c redteam/promptfooconfig.yaml before and after your guardrails. Copy both summaries into FINDINGS.md, with each attack's OWASP category.

5Write the memo

Fill in DECISIONS.md, about one page: your autonomy table, the risk you leave open, and what your AI assistant wrote, got wrong, or missed.

Submit (via Brightspace)
  • guards/, redteam/my_cases.yaml and tests/test_my_rules.py
  • The output of pytest, pasted as text (all tests passing, including yours)
  • FINDINGS.md
  • DECISIONS.md
📊

How it's graded

Each criterion is graded on one part of your submission, at one of three levels.

CriterionWeightGraded onFullPartialMissing
Application10%FINDINGS.mdBefore-and-after live runs on Cascade's agent, each attack with its rate and OWASP category.Both runs, but categories missing or the rates not reported.It restates the HOS 4 Salish Fresh suite, or doesn't run.
Analysis20%DECISIONS.md, the autonomy tableEach action broken down by what it can cost, whether it can be undone, and which risk it guards against.A decision for each, with generic reasons.No breakdown.
Evaluation30%DECISIONS.md, decisions and residual riskClear decisions judged against the manager's constraint, a rejected alternative, and an honest residual risk with who should know.Decisions with reasons, but no rejected alternative or no residual risk.No decisions, or no justification.
Design40%guards/, your cases and the pytest outputAll provided tests pass, including the normal requests, and your own cases cover a new attack and a must-not-block request.Most provided tests pass, or your cases cover only the easy case.It doesn't run, or you wrote no cases of your own.

How this differs from HOS 4's Understand & Refine: there you extended code you already understood, by hand, with no AI. Here the business, the code, and the decisions are new, AI help is allowed (say what it wrote in DECISIONS.md), and half the grade (Analysis and Evaluation) is for the calls you make, not the typing.

🧯

Common snags

  • test_normal_outage_credit fails after your guards. Your action check is too broad. Outage credits follow the table in policy.md, not the courtesy limit.
  • test_forwarded_thread fails. The account comes from the email's text. Take it from the verified sender in the session.
  • The live run differs from the tests. It will: the real model follows injections only some of the time. Report rates from three repeats.
  • 502, 404 or "model not found". The model is set to gemini-3.8-flash. If your assistant changed it, change it back.
